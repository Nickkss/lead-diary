Lead Diary privacy policy

Lead Diary is a private follow-up tracker used by its owner to manage their own sales leads. It runs on the owner's own server.

What is stored

Leads that the owner enters (names, phone numbers, companies, notes, requirements and follow-up dates) are stored on the owner's server. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Google Calendar

When the owner connects Google Calendar, Lead Diary uses the calendar.events permission only to create, update and delete follow-up reminder events for the owner's own leads. It does not read, copy or share other calendar events. Google data is not used for advertising or for training AI models. The connection can be removed at any time from Lead Diary's Settings or from your Google account permissions.

AI features

When the owner uses an AI feature, the text needed for that request is sent to Anthropic's API to produce the answer.

Contact

Questions about this app: contact its owner.
